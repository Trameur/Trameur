🧬 Privacy Policy

Subject 264

Last updated: June 16, 2026

TL;DR: the game collects no personal data. Distribution runs through Steam.

About

Subject 264 is a single-player, VR-only first-person escape game distributed on Steam.

Data collection

The game itself does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account system inside the game and no analytics or advertising SDK. Save data and settings are kept locally on your computer.

Distribution platform (Steam)

The game is sold and delivered through Steam. Your purchase, downloads, playtime and any achievements are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal Steam account details.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game removes its local save data from your computer.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
