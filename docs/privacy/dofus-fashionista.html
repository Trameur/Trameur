🎨 Privacy Policy

Dofus Fashionista

Last updated: June 16, 2026

TL;DR: we only store what's needed to save your builds. No selling of data, ever.

Who we are

Dofus Fashionista (dofusfashionista.gg) is a free equipment advisor for the game Dofus. It is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with Ankama. "We" refers to the maintainer of Dofus Fashionista.

Data we collect

  • Account data (optional): if you create an account, we store a username, an email address and a hashed password so you can log in and find your saved builds.
  • Builds & preferences: the character builds, items and settings you create are stored so they're available the next time you log in.
  • Technical logs: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be recorded temporarily for security and to keep the service running.

You can browse and use core features without an account; in that case your builds may be kept only in your browser.

Cookies

The site uses functional cookies required to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences.

How we use your data

Only to operate the service: authenticate you, save and display your builds, and keep the site secure and reliable. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Your personal data is not shared with third parties, except service providers strictly needed to run the site (e.g. hosting), and only as required to keep it working or to comply with the law.

Data retention & deletion

Account data and builds are kept as long as your account exists. You can ask to delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Children

The service is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: trameurlabs@gmail.com

Dofus Fashionista is a fan project, not affiliated with Ankama Games.
