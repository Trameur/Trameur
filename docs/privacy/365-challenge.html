📅 Privacy Policy

365 Challenge

Last updated: June 16, 2026

TL;DR: everything stays on your phone.

Data collection

365 Challenge does not collect, transmit, store or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no third-party service.

Data storage

Your challenges, progress and history are stored locally on your device only (AsyncStorage). They never leave your phone unless you explicitly use the "Export my data" feature, which hands the data to an app of your choosing via the system share sheet.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): used only to show the daily reminder you configure. Scheduled locally; nothing is sent to any server.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all its data. Within the app, deleting a challenge removes it; the anonymous activity journal remains on your device until you uninstall.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
